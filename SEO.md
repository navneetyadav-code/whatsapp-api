1. On your GitHub repo page, click **Releases** &rarr; **Draft a new release**.
2. **Tag version:** `v1.0.0` (Create new tag on publish).
3. **Target:** `main`.
4. **Release title:**

5. **Release Description:**
